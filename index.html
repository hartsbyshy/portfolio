hello, welcome to my little creative corner

Hi, I’m Shy.

I’m a freelance illustrator and designer who loves making things feel thoughtful, playful, and human.

My creative journey started with illustration, but curiosity has slowly taken me into graphic design, branding, motion, web design, and now UI/UX.

I’m still discovering where creativity can take me — experimenting with new tools, learning new skills, and finding new ways to turn little ideas into things people can see, use, and experience.

This portfolio is a little collection of that journey: polished pieces, experiments, and ideas I simply wanted to bring to life.
